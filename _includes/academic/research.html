01 / RESEARCH

From equations
to biological insight.

My research focuses on nonlinear dynamical systems: what their measurements can tell us, which parameters can be determined, and how their behaviour can be predicted and controlled.

At the University of Vigo, I study structural identifiability and observability under the supervision of Alejandro F. Villaverde, with José Faro Rivas as co-supervisor for applications in immunology.

Understanding what can be learned from a model before fitting it to data. My work includes benchmarking analysis tools, discrete symmetries and STRIKE-GOLDD.
