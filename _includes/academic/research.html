01 / RESEARCH

Research areas

My research focuses on nonlinear dynamical systems: what their measurements can tell us, which parameters can be determined, and how their behaviour can be predicted and controlled.

I am a postdoctoral researcher at the University of Vigo, with a PhD in Mathematical Methods and Numerical Simulation. My doctoral research brought together structural identifiability, observability and mathematical immunology, with Alejandro F. Villaverde and José Faro Rivas as joint supervisors.
